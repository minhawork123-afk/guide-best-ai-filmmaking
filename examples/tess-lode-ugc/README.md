Key Decisions — Why They Were Made
No music. UGC authenticity lives in silence and room tone. Music signals "ad."
Phone-shot look, not cinematic. The whole film is framed as self-recorded content.
Hard directional daylight in the gym functions as a practical key —
the gym cinematography is real, it just looks like someone propped their phone well.
Low energy performance. "Talking to her phone like she's talking to a friend."
No enthusiasm, no smiling at the lens. The more understated, the more credible.
Two location plates, one gym. Plate A is the wide establishing shot for her arrival.
Plate B is the filming bay corner where she actually records — a subset of the same space,
with the window daylight doing real work as a key light.
Product reference as @Image2. The LODE tub and shaker get their own reference image
so Seedance can match the product exactly in close-up shots without reinventing it.
